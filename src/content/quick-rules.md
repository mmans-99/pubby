# Pubby — Quick rules

Short versions of the rules, shown in the in-game help. Must match `PROJECT.md`.

## How to win a round
Get rid of all your cards. First out is 1st. The last player still holding cards loses the round.

## Playing a trick
The leader plays any card. Going clockwise, everyone must play the same suit if they can. Highest card of that suit leads next. Ace is high.

## Slaps
No card of the led suit? Play any other card. That's a SLAP! The trick stops, and whoever played the highest card of the led suit picks up every card in the trick, then leads.

## Jokers
Wild. Play one on your turn, even if you could follow suit. It skips your turn and then leaves the game. If you lead with a joker, the next player starts the trick. You can't hold only jokers: when you play your last normal card, any jokers left are thrown away and that was your last card. 2 jokers with 2 to 5 players, 3 with 6 to 8. The host can turn jokers off.

## Missing a suit
No cards of a suit after the deal? Say so. You lead first. If more than one player is missing a suit, play Reaction Tap: the slowest tapper leads. Tapping early counts as slowest.

## The last two players
When only two players hold cards and you play your last card:
- Lower than theirs, or a slap: you win the round.
- Higher than theirs: pick up the whole waste pile and keep playing (you can still forfeit later), or forfeit and take last place.
Both go out in the same trick? Lower card or slapper wins.
While the other player is down to their last normal card, you can't play a joker: play a normal card.

## Taking cards
Before a trick starts, you can ask to take all of another player's cards (unseen). One request at a time. They agree or refuse; if several players ask the same person, they choose who gets them. The giver goes out and takes the next place. Not when only two players are left.

## Hidden swap (2 and 3 players)
At the start of every round: swap up to 4 of your cards for random unseen ones, or keep your hand. Swapped-out cards leave the round.

## Votes
More than half wins. A tie goes to the host. Votes happen before a round (reshuffle, once per round) or on the results screen (target score, Ultimate Round).

## Ultimate Round
One final round for glory, worth no points. The winner beats every score; the loser ranks below every score. The game ends after it.
